How do I get an API key?
  1. Log in to clist.by.
  2. Open the API page and follow the first line to get the key.
  3. Copy the username:key part of the Request header line into the field above.
How are ratings calculated?

Ratings come from two sources: zerotrac and clist.by. Both use Elo: a problem's rating is the rating of a person who solves it half the time.

zerotrac ratings are shown as plain numbers. Weekly contests 1-62 are not covered.

clist.by ratings are marked with c, like 1275c, and fill in problems zerotrac does not cover. It counts people with more past contests more heavily. It also looks at how well each person did in that contest, not only at their rating before it. So a c rating and a plain rating can differ for problems of the same difficulty.
